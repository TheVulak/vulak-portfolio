INFO

About me

I've been a Blockbench artist since 2024. I've been creating projects and collaborating on established ones for a while now. I enjoy designing armor and armor. I think one of my best skills is using color theory and visual representation when creating my models.

I'm a big fan of rock, metal, folk and knightcore. Personally, if you have a medieval-themed request, I'd be incredibly happy.

Workflow

How I usually do my models

Idea & Concept definition

You give me the idea, the format, and what it's for, and I'll adjust it to your needs as much as possible.

Model sketch

Once the idea is defined, I'll make you a simple sketch of the model and its main structure, and you can tell me what you think.

Final details

Once you give me your feedback, I'll finalize the details and you'll have your model ready to add to your project.

Contact me

Tell me what you have in mind.

I'm open to bringing the ideas from your mind to your world, or even beyond.
